# TITLE: The Bench That Beat Benny
# FORMAT: B, learning through a story
# LENGTH: about 60 s, 135 spoken words
# QUESTION: How will Benny get his bench outside when doing everything alone keeps failing?
# ANSWERED AT: "Berry, the door? Cherry, the other end?"
# CAST: berry, benny, cherry
# PLACE: house doorway and the adjoining garden, dusk
# TONE: Warm comedy about pride and accepting help; no spoken moral.
# CONTINUITY: Benny has already built a simple bench; the doorway is wide enough, but its door swings closed when released.

# SEE: Benny holds one end of a bench while the half-closed door blocks its other end.
benny: Why can't I move this stupid bench?
berry: Want a hand?
benny: No. I built it myself. I'll move it myself.
cherry: Where are we moving it?
benny: Outside. Best spot in the garden. Before sunset.

# SEE: Benny releases the bench to open the door, which swings shut as he picks the bench up again.

berry: I don't think doors learn commands.
benny: This one will. We've spent enough time together.

# SEE: Benny holds the door with one foot but cannot reach the bench's far end.

benny: I've still got a perfectly good chin.
berry: Please keep that attached.

# SEE: Benny sits on the stranded bench as the warm patch of sunlight shrinks outside.
benny: I wanted you to see something I'd done all by myself.
cherry: We saw. It's a lovely bench.
benny: It looks less impressive stuck in a doorway.
berry: Only the moving part is stuck.
benny: Feels like the whole thing.

# SEE: Cherry holds the door for Berry to carry a small watering can outside.
benny: You didn't even think about asking.
berry: I had full hands. She had a free one.

# SEE: Benny stands at one end of the bench and looks directly at his friends.
benny: Berry, the door? Cherry, the other end?
cherry: Ready when you are.
benny: Together. Lift.

# SEE: The three sit on the bench in the garden as the last sunlight reaches their shoes.
berry: Still your bench.
benny: Better with people on it.
