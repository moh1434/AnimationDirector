# Title: The World's Unluckiest Thief

**[Scene 1 – The Perfect Plan]**

Meet Bob.

Bob is a professional thief... well, at least he thinks he is.

Tonight, he's planning the biggest robbery of his life.

He studies the house, puts on his black mask, grabs his tools, and whispers:

"Easy money!"

**[Scene 2 – The First Disaster]**

Bob sneaks through an open window.

Everything is going perfectly...

Until his pants get caught on a nail!

He struggles, kicks, and finally falls straight into a flower pot.

But somehow, he manages to get inside.

**[Scene 3 – Things Get Worse]**

Bob spots a giant diamond sitting on a table.

Jackpot!

He reaches for it...

BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!

The security alarm goes off!

Panicking, Bob runs into the nearest cupboard and hides.

**[Scene 4 – The Worst Hiding Spot]**

He slowly opens the cupboard door...

And comes face-to-face with a gigantic sleeping dog!

Bob freezes.

The dog's eyes slowly open.

Bob closes his eyes and whispers:

"Please don't bark..."

The dog suddenly jumps on him!

But instead of attacking, it starts licking his face.

It thinks Bob is its new best friend!

**[Scene 5 – The Final Disaster]**

Bob escapes through the front door, covered in dog slobber.

He runs down the street, celebrating his survival.

"I DID IT! I'M FREE!"

But suddenly...

A police car pulls up.

The officer steps out and shouts:

"YOU! HANDS UP!"

Bob freezes.

**[Scene 6 – The Twist]**

The officer looks at him and says:

"You're under arrest for stealing... my dog!"

Bob looks down.

The giant dog is happily following him, carrying his stolen bag!

Bob slowly turns toward the camera.

"Can this night get any worse?"

**[END]**

# Visual direction:

Opening: Make Bob look like a highly skilled criminal. Dramatic music, serious facial expression, and exaggerated stealth movements.

Comedy: Use sudden sound effects, fast zooms, awkward pauses, and exaggerated body movements.

Best scene: The dog should be enormous compared to Bob, but extremely friendly. Contrast his terrified expression with the dog's excitement.

Ending: Pause before the officer's accusation. Let the audience expect Bob to be arrested for burglary, then reveal the ridiculous reason.
