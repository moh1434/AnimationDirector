# The Dishonest Business Partner
Character "A" helps his friend plant mint in the garden. Later, he also helps him plant lemon trees.

His friend is very happy with his help and says:

"You're a great partner! Why don't we start a small lemonade business together?"

Character "A" agrees, and they begin selling fresh lemon juice.

But whenever Character "A" sells the lemonade, he secretly keeps some of the money instead of sharing all the earnings with his friend.

At first, nobody notices. But one day, his friend checks the accounts and discovers the missing money.

He says: `You was realy nice and helped me grow the lemons, but later You changed. You become greedy and stole my money!`
