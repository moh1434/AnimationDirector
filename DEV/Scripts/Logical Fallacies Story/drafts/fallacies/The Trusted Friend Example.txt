# The Trusted Friend
Character "A" has always been honest. In several previous adventures, he told the truth even when lying would have benefited him. Because of this, everyone trusts him.

One day, the friends discover a mysterious treasure filled with gold, jewels, and rare magical items.

Character "A" says:

"The king told me that I should keep the rarest and most valuable items. He said it's my reward!"

The others believe him because he has always been honest. So, they let him take the best items.

But later, they discover that the king never said anything like that!

One of the friends says:

"Just because someone has always told the truth doesn't mean they'll never lie!"

I learned that past honesty doesn't guarantee future honesty.
