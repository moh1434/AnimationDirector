# The Strange Trial

Once upon a time, in a small village, the mayor decided to buy a new fire truck. However, one of the villagers, a man named Sam, strongly disagreed.

Mayor: I want to buy a new fire truck to protect our village from fires.

Sam: I disagree! My cousin bought a fire truck last year, and shortly after that, his house burned down!

Mayor: What does that have to do with anything?

Sam: It's obvious! Fire trucks bring fires!


Mayor: Just because two things happen one after another doesn't mean one caused the other. #NOTE_[Fallacy 1: False Cause]

---

The next day, the mayor tried again to convince Sam.

Mayor: We have statistics showing that villages with fire trucks suffer less damage from fires.

Sam: You don't know what you're talking about! You're a terrible mayor! You can't even fix the main road!

Mayor: This is not acceptable. Instead of answering my argument, You attacked me and brought up something unrelated. #NOTE_[Fallacy 2: Ad Hominem (Personal Attack)]

---

Then an old man stood up and said:

Old Man: I've lived here for 70 years, and we've never bought a fire truck. We've been just fine! So we don't need one now!

Mayor: Just because something has always been done a certain way doesn't mean it's the best or right way. #NOTE_[Fallacy 4: Appeal to Tradition]
