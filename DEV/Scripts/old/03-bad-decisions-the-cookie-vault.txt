# TITLE: The Benny-Proof Cookie Vault
# FORMAT: C, bad decision, funny consequence
# LENGTH: about 45 s, 100 spoken words
# QUESTION: Can Benny's elaborate cookie security protect the cookies from Benny?
# ANSWERED AT: "I tested it after every improvement."
# CAST: berry, benny
# PLACE: kitchen
# TONE: Affectionate satire of overengineering and finding excuses; the joke is about Benny's system, not food or body size.
# STAGING: Show the box and one security prop at a time; cuts replace fine hand movements and lock manipulation.

# SEE: Benny proudly guards a small closed cookie box with a padlock.
benny: These cookies are finally Benny-proof.
benny: They're for our big picnic. I'm protecting them until tomorrow.
berry: Where's the key?
benny: My pocket. For emergencies.
berry: Such as?
benny: Finding out whether the lock works.

# SEE: The box is open and Benny holds a cookie beside the unlocked padlock.
berry: You've already opened it.
benny: Security testing. I found a serious weakness.
berry: The pocket?
benny: The pocket.

# SEE: The key sits on a high shelf while Benny looks satisfied below it.
benny: There. Impossible to reach.
berry: And the stool?
benny: Unrelated furniture.

# SEE: Benny stands beside the stool with another cookie and the retrieved key.
berry: How's the security business?
benny: Constantly evolving. Unfortunately, so is the criminal.

# SEE: Benny hands Berry the key while the box sits closed between them.
benny: You keep this. Whatever I say, don't give it back.
berry: Finally. How many cookies did we save?
benny: Let me check.
berry: I'll check.

# SEE: Berry holds up the open box, revealing only crumbs.
berry: Benny.
benny: I tested it after every improvement.
berry: You ate the entire security budget.
