# Family bible: Benny, Berry, Wendy and Cherry

*It Made Sense at the Time* (working title): a loving family in which everyone is smart in one way and confidently wrong in another, and each day somebody's perfectly reasonable idea makes a mess.

This file says who they are. [RULES.md](RULES.md) says how to write an episode. Only the [series log](SERIES_LOG.md) says what has happened on screen. The reasons behind all three are in the [series plan](../SERIES_PLAN.md).

## The situation

They share a home in the present day. The series starts on the day Benny quits his job as a chef in someone else's restaurant. From that day he cooks at home: a cloud restaurant, delivery only, with no dining room. Customers find him through his social media page, and the delivery guy takes the food to them on a motorcycle. Introduce each of these on screen before an episode relies on it.

- **Berry** is an independent artist and creative entrepreneur. She makes oil paintings (portraits, landscapes, decorative pieces) and handmade jewelry at home and sells them online through her own social media page.
- **Wendy** finished high school and is taking a gap year before college. She has no job for now, and what she will study is undecided.
- **Cherry** goes to elementary school and keeps a diary.

The house needs money now, and everyone works, studies and argues in the same rooms.

## Four ways of thinking

Each person's strength and flaw are the same trait. The flaw explains what they do and never excuses it. Consequences last.

| | Benny, 41 | Berry, 39 | Wendy, 18 | Cherry, 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Thinks with** | Hope | Stories | Proof | Deals |
| **Motto never said aloud** | If it feels right, it's right | I can see exactly what this means | If you can't measure it, it isn't there | There's always a way to yes |
| **Strength** | Tries things, forgives fast | Notices what others miss | Checks | Asks what nobody dares ask |
| **The same trait as a flaw** | Counts the hits, forgets the misses | Fills gaps with a story and believes it | Thinks she is the only one without bias | Bends any rule toward what she wants |
| **Often goes wrong with** | Survivorship bias, false cause, sunk cost, planning fallacy, gambler's fallacy | Confirmation bias, mind reading, false dilemma, slippery slope, appeal to emotion | Bias blind spot, overconfidence, fallacy fallacy, false precision, curse of knowledge | Bandwagon, special pleading, whataboutism, moving goalposts, loaded question |
| **Wants** | To be the one who made it work | To be understood without explaining | To be right | To be counted as a full person |
| **Needs** | To be loved on a day it didn't work | To ask instead of guess | To be wrong and still be fine | To say plainly what she wants |
| **When caught** | Jokes, then goes quiet | Defends, then says it straight | Corrects your wording | Renegotiates |
| **Running bit** | Announces good news that isn't | "I'm not upset" (she has already cleaned a whole room) | Charts on the fridge | Contracts in crayon |

The lists of mistakes are habits, not limits: anyone can make any mistake, and **anyone can be the one who sees it**. No family member is the teacher, the smart one or the one who is always right. Wendy checks things, and she is wrong as often as the others.

Cherry is the one who visibly learns. Over time she recognizes a mistake she has seen before, and sometimes uses it on purpose to get her way.

### The people

**Benny (`benny`), father.** Warm, generous, easily taken with a plan. Embarrassment makes him talk more; a joke is often his first offer of peace. He finds it hard to say no to a customer, takes a bad review personally, and wants the people he loves to have something to look forward to.

**Berry (`berry`), mother.** Observant, direct, imaginative. She can make an ordinary afternoon feel like an occasion. She reads a whole story in one raised eyebrow, and she is right often enough to trust it. She finds it hard to put a price on a piece or let a favorite one go.

**Wendy (`wendy`), older daughter.** Calm, spare with words, dryly funny. She wants to do her gap year correctly, which means she keeps comparing futures instead of picking one. She may start solving your problem before hearing it. She is sometimes the last to realize she owes somebody an apology.

**Cherry (`cherry`), younger daughter.** Notices who waited, whose turn got skipped and what was promised, word for word. She loves being trusted with a real job. She argues like a tiny lawyer and means every word.

**The delivery guy (no speaker ID): reality, on a motorcycle.** He never speaks and has no name yet. When he arrives, the consequence arrives with him, and a look at him can be the punchline. Orange jacket and helmet, blue motorcycle. He can only be drawn on the motorcycle (props `delivery_rider`, `delivery_rider_art`, and their `_waiting` versions).

Keep the established looks. Wendy has a dark purple bob with a high side ponytail, a yellow bow, purple cardigan, white collared shirt and dark purple skirt. Benny wears a yellow hoodie, grey cargo trousers and yellow sneakers, with brown hair. Berry has berry-pink bun hair with a star clip, a blush blouse with a star brooch, and a long rose-pink skirt. Cherry has a dark brown ponytail with a cherry clip, a red hoodie and a black skirt.

## What happens when they collide

| Pair | Why it is funny | Why we care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benny and Berry | One jumps while the other imagines the landing | They still laugh together after the kids are in bed |
| Benny and Wendy | Hope against proof: neither ever convinces the other | He tells people her news too early because he is proud |
| Benny and Cherry | He says yes, and she has it in writing | She remembers the small plan he mentioned in passing |
| Berry and Wendy | A story against a chart about the same event | They can sit quietly at one table for hours |
| Berry and Cherry | Both can build a case out of nothing | Each sees things about the other that nobody else does |
| Wendy and Cherry | Evidence against lawyering | A closed door still hurts, and an apology still counts |

They tease. They never despise each other, and they always like each other. Children can be right, go quiet and change their minds. A parent who pushed unfairly takes responsibility without asking the child to comfort them.

Side characters need a want of their own. Give a speaker a short lower-case ID, and record recurring details after approval.
