# SEE: A trolley wheel catches on the cushion of a chair by the café door.
ruth: Could this chair move six inches?
benny: Ruth, that's my aunt's favourite spot. She likes to watch the street.
ruth: So do I. Usually from the other side of that door.
benny: First morning I've had all the seats full. People need somewhere to sit.

# SEE: Benny picks up the chair and points at the row behind it.
benny: If we take away all the seats, what sort of café is left?
ruth: I didn't ask for all the seats. I didn't even ask for this one. /** Fallacy 1: Straw man */
ruth: Can we try what I actually asked for?

# SEE: Ruth points to a patch of floor; Benny slides the chair across.
benny: Six inches toward the window. Go on, try the trolley now.
ruth: Look at that. Both wheels inside and your aunt can still sit down.
benny: You're right. I argued with somebody who wasn't here.

# SEE: Ruth retrieves the cushion from her trolley.
ruth: Good. When she gets here, tell her I saved her seat.
