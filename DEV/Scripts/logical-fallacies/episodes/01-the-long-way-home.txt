# STATUS: approved
# TITLE: The Long Way Home
# ARC: standalone
# LENGTH: about 100 s, 175 spoken words
# WANT: Berry has been looking forward to a little time alone before the girls get home.
# CAST: berry, benny
# PLACE: the car on the way home from buying groceries, the kitchen, then the front step that evening

# SEE: From the passenger seat, Berry watches their usual turn slip past.
berry: That was our street.
benny: I know. Thought we'd go by the swings.
berry: What for?
benny: Nothing. It's been a while.

# SEE: The swings come into view. For two seconds Berry watches a father lift a sleeping child from one.
berry: You used to duck driving past here.
benny: Cherry could spot those swings dead asleep.
berry: And there you'd be, pushing her in her pajamas.
benny: You always said one more push.
berry: She had the hiccups from laughing. I liked that.

# SEE: Berry smiles until she catches sight of the dashboard clock.
benny: We could go round by the school, too.
berry: Benny, could you take this next left?
benny: Yeah. Sure.

# SEE: He signals. Hold on Berry for three seconds as she turns to say something and he concentrates on the turn.

# SEE: Cut to the kitchen. Benny puts apples in the bowl while Berry stands with her coat still on.
berry: I did like going past the swings.

# SEE: Benny puts the last apple in the bowl. Give him two quiet seconds before he looks up.
benny: I should've asked.
benny: I just miss talking in the car.
berry: I've been thinking about that front step since lunch.
benny: The step?
berry: It's still warm around now. The girls won't be back till five.
berry: I wanted to take my shoes off and sit there.
benny: Oh. And I drove right past it.
berry: Twice, if you'd had your way.

# SEE: That gets a little laugh. Benny takes the milk from her and Berry leaves her shoes by the door.

# SEE: Cut to dusk. The girls' shoes are beside Berry's now. Benny opens the door and holds out Berry's sweater.
benny: Thought you might want this.
berry: Get yours too?

# SEE: Berry pulls on her sweater. When Benny comes back wearing his, she shifts over. Give them four seconds to settle side by side.
berry: You know what I miss about those drives?
benny: What?
berry: We used to pick a house we'd buy.
benny: Little green one with the crooked porch?
berry: You picked that one every time.

# SEE: Hold for three seconds as they look across the street together, Berry's shoulder resting against his.

# LOG UPDATE
# Benny and Berry used to take longer drives when the girls were small and pick out houses together. Benny usually chose a green house with a crooked porch.
# Berry tells Benny she sometimes counts on sitting alone on the warm front step before the girls come home.
